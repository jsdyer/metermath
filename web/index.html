Source & assumptions

Rates are in Canadian dollars. Prices prorate the hourly meter charge between 9 a.m. and 10 p.m. App fees, payment increments, live vacancies, temporary signs and event calendars are outside this snapshot. Current signs and meter displays govern the visit.

Nearby stadium results flag a 650 m screening radius for potential event conditions. This radius is approximate. The city determines event locations and times. A gas motorcycle paying through PayByPhone receives the city’s half-rate in regular spaces.
